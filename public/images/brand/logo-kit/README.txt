================================

IMPORTANT ASSET LIMITATIONS
---------------------------
These assets are raster crops from an AI-generated presentation board. The surrounding cream, emerald or black panel backgrounds are retained in many crops; they are NOT transparent logos. The files are not editable vector artwork and should not be treated as final print-production masters. Small source crops have limited native detail; upscaling does not add real detail.

Before production use, have the selected mark redrawn and approved as clean vector artwork (SVG, PDF/EPS) and export true transparent PNGs, print-ready CMYK files, favicon/app assets and single-colour variants. Check exact spelling, letterforms and legibility at small sizes. The board is a creative concept, not a trademark clearance or proof of logo originality.
